# Changelog

All notable public changes across **Lumo for macOS** and **Lumo for iOS & iPadOS** are documented here.

---

## macOS v1.5.0 — November 2026

### Highlights
- **Editorial Dark Mode & Refined Surface Tokens** — Full dark theme (`#141413` warm obsidian canvas, calibrated contrast tokens, and flash-free startup synchronization) alongside Lumo's signature warm paper light theme.
- **Structured Workflow Intent Bar** — Clicking **Create Slides**, **Create Content**, **Plan Content Calendar**, or **Create Video Cover** now opens an interactive intent bar with aspect-ratio, platform, and style chips instead of inserting verbose boilerplate into the prompt box.
- **Complete 2×2 Creator Workflow Grid** — Added first-class **Create Video Cover** access directly on the Welcome canvas alongside Slides, Content Packs, and Content Calendar planning.
- **Unified Lumo Flash & Lumo Pro Selector** — Streamlined model selection across Composer and `/flash` · `/pro` slash commands out of the box.
- **Cloud Media & Voice Upgrade** — Upgraded cloud cover image generation, cloud Whisper speech-to-text, and curated bilingual read-aloud voices (`Default`, `Asteria`, `Luna`, `Arcas`, `Orion`) with unified plan quota accounting.
- **Complete 6-Language Localization** — Full UI coverage across English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, and Português across Slides Studio, Cover Studio, Library, and Voice Settings.

---

## macOS v1.4.0 & iOS v1.4.0 — November 2026

### Highlights
- **Apple App Store Subscriptions & Unified Plans** — Seamless plan management across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
- **Workspace-Aware Cloud Agent & Relay Continuity** — Improved connection resilience, low-latency WebSocket downlink, and explicit boundaries between **Local Mac**, **Remote Mac**, and **Cloud Agent**.
- **Slides Studio & Cover Editor Polish** — Improved `.pptx` export fidelity, layout QA checks, and multi-ratio cover previews.
